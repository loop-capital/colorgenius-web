# Soap Cap (Color Balancer / Bleach Wash)

> A gentle way to lift out or soften artificial color without full bleach application.

## Recipe

| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Lightener (e.g., Lanza Decolorizer) | 1 scoop |
| Shampoo (clarifying preferred) | 1 scoop |
| Developer (20 vol fine hair, 30 vol if more lift needed) | Equal parts to lightener + shampoo |

**Mix ratio: 1 part lightener + 1 part shampoo + equal parts developer**

## Application

1. Wet the hair — damp, not dripping
2. Apply evenly — work through colored areas like shampooing
3. Massage in — emulsify through hair for even distribution
4. Check every 5 minutes
5. Processing time: 10–20 minutes max
6. Rinse when ready — don't over-process

## Rules

- **Never** apply to dry hair — always damp
- **Never** use over 30 vol
- **Never** leave on too long — controlled lift only
- Check frequently — artificial color lifts unpredictably
- Condition after with treatment

## Use When

- Removing or softening box color before new application
- Correcting banding from previous color
- Pre-lifting before base break or highlights
- Gently evening out uneven color deposits

## Do NOT Use When

- Hair is already compromised/damaged
- As a replacement for proper color correction
- Hair has metallic dye buildup (some box colors)
