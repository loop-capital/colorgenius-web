# L'ANZA Healing Color - Complete Shade Database

## Product Overview
L'ANZA Healing Color Cream is a professional hair color system featuring:
- **Keratin Healing System** and **Flower Shield Complex**
- 3-in-1 versatile formulation (Permanent, Demi-Permanent, Semi-Permanent)
- Gray coverage up to 100%
- Processing time: 30 minutes (up to 40 minutes for resistant gray)
- Mix ratio: 1:1.5 (1 part color + 1.5 parts developer)

## Developer Options
- 10 Volume (3%) - Deposit only, gray coverage
- 20 Volume (6%) - Standard lift (1-2 levels)
- 30 Volume (9%) - Maximum lift (2-3 levels)
- 40 Volume (12%) - Super lift

## Color Theory & Mixing Guidelines

### Gray Coverage
- **0-25% Gray**: Use N series
- **25-50% Gray**: Mix N + target shade (equal parts)
- **50-100% Gray**: Use NN series + N series (equal parts)
- Processing time: 30-40 minutes for resistant gray

### Lifting Guidelines
- **1 level**: 10 Volume (3%) developer
- **2 levels**: 20 Volume (6%) developer
- **3 levels**: 30 Volume (9%) developer
- **Super lift**: 40 Volume (12%) developer + high lift shades

### Tonal Direction
- **Cool tones**: Ash (A), Violet (V), Pearl (P), Natural Ash (NA), Extra Ash (AX)
- **Warm tones**: Gold (G), Copper (C), Red (R), Beige (B)
- **Neutral tones**: Natural (N), Double Natural (NN)
- **Fashion/Specialty**: Titanium (T), Specialty shades, Kickers

### Corrective Formulation
- **Too warm**: Add ash or violet tones
- **Too cool**: Add gold or copper tones
- **Brassy**: Use violet or blue-based tones
- **Too dark**: Use lighter level with same tone
- **Too light**: Use darker level with same tone
