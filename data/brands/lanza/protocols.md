# Lanza Healing Color — Professional Protocols

## Gray Coverage Protocol

- **50% gray or more** → Use **Double Natural (NN) series**
  - Example: 6NN for level 6 with heavy gray
  - NN series provides maximum gray coverage and deposit on resistant strands
  - Optional ash kick: Mix 6NN (2 parts) + 6A (1 part) to keep cool-leaning while anchoring gray

## Toner Application

- **Ratio: 1 part color + 1 part translucent + 2 parts demi**
- Processing time: 15–20 minutes
- Gives controlled, sheer deposit without over-depositing on pre-lightened hair
- Translucent prevents muddiness
- Demi adds shine + longevity

## Lightening

- Use **Lanza Decolorizer** for bleach applications
- For fine hair: 20 vol developer
- For more lift needed: 30 vol max

## Aftercare

- Lanza Keratin Healing Oil post-service
- Purple shampoo recommendation for cool tone maintenance
