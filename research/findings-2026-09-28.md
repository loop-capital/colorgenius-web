### 1. L'Oréal × OpenAI Major Partnership Expands to Hair/Makeup Try-On in ChatGPT
- **Source:** https://www.europesays.com/france/73264/ (confirmed via L'Oréal press release, Marketplace.org)
- **Title:** "L'Oréal and OpenAI Join Forces for Transformation in Beauty with AI"
- **Relevance:** HIGH
- **Summary:** Announced at VivaTech 2026 (early September), L'Oréal and OpenAI deepened their partnership across two strategic areas: (1) AI-powered consumer journeys and commerce, and (2) AI-powered applications across research, science, and marketing. First initiatives include Maybelline New York integrating virtual makeup try-on into ChatGPT using L'Oréal's ModiFace technology. Lancôme and Kérastase (U.S.) will enhance product discovery within ChatGPT. On the research side, OpenAI's GPT-Rosalind life sciences reasoning model will map the skin microbiome for La Roche-Posay development. CreAItech, L'Oréal's in-house generative AI content platform, will also be powered by OpenAI models.
- **Product Relevance:** This is a significant competitive signal. L'Oréal now has the world's most advanced AI infrastructure in beauty (ModiFace + OpenAI + ChatGPT distribution). Their virtual try-on technology entering ChatGPT means consumers will increasingly expect AI-driven hair/makeup previews as a baseline. ColorGenius's professional-only positioning is the right differentiator, but the consumer-side pressure will rise. L'Oréal has not yet targeted professional formulation generation, which remains our gap.
- **Action Needed:** Monitor whether L'Oréal expands ModiFace into professional salon tools or formula recommendation. Document our multi-brand conversion advantage vs. L'Oréal's single-brand lock-in. Consider whether ChatGPT plugin or API integration could be a future distribution channel for ColorGenius.
