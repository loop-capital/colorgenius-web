## Category 1: Hair Colorimetry & Dye Chemistry (NEW — Sept 2026)

### Finding 1: Herbal Hair Dye Shampoo with Instrumental Colorimetry Validation
- **Source:** https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9284/13/5/222 (Cosmetics, MDPI, August 2026)
- **Relevance:** MEDIUM
- **Summary:** A study on herbal-based hair dye shampoos evaluated coloration performance using instrumental colorimetry (L*a*b* values) to measure color durability and hair morphology changes. The research demonstrates that colorimetric measurement (Delta E calculations) is now standard even for novel/natural dye formulations, validating our approach of using CIELAB for formula matching.
- **Product Relevance:** Confirms that CIELAB-based measurement is the industry-standard quantitative method for evaluating hair dye performance across all formulation types (chemical, herbal, natural). Supports ColorGenius's color-matching engine architecture.
- **Action Needed:** None immediate. Reference this study in any documentation about why we use CIELAB for hair color measurement.

### Finding 2: Fe3+-Mediated Polyphenol Coatings on Hair with Colorimetric Evaluation
- **Source:** https://www.mdpi.com/2223-7747/15/16/2500 (Plants, MDPI, August 2026)
- **Relevance:** MEDIUM
- **Summary:** Researchers used Aronia melanocarpa extract to create Fe3+-mediated interfacial polyphenol coatings on hair fibers. Instrumental colorimetry was the primary quantitative method for evaluating dyeing performance. The study emphasizes that colorimetric measurement is sufficient for objectively assessing natural dye deposition on hair.
- **Product Relevance:** Growing body of research using instrumental colorimetry for natural/plant-based hair dye evaluation. As ColorGenius expands to cover natural/organic color lines, this validates our measurement approach for those formulations too.
- **Action Needed:** None immediate. Keep on file as evidence that colorimetry applies broadly across dye chemistry types.

### Finding 3: Heavy Metal Contamination in Cosmetic Hair Dyes — Safety Assessment
- **Source:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S221475002600137X (Journal of Cosmetic Science / ScienceDirect, August 2026)
- **Relevance:** HIGH
- **Summary:** Integrated study combining product contamination data, biomonitoring, and dermal risk assessment for heavy metals in cosmetic hair dyes among women in Sudan. Published in a peer-reviewed cosmetic science journal, it represents the type of safety research that drives regulatory scrutiny of hair color ingredients.
- **Product Relevance:** Regulatory pressure on hair dye safety continues globally. ColorGenius's ingredient database should flag heavy metal risk levels by brand/line. Could be a trust/safety differentiator for salons concerned about product safety.
- **Action Needed:** Review whether our brand database includes any heavy metal or contaminant risk flags. Consider adding safety metadata to shade entries.

## Category 4: Colorimetry Standards & Measurement Technology (September 2026)

### Finding 4: ISO 7724 Colorimetry Standard — Portable Spectrophotometer Review
- **Source:** https://www.ledphotometer.com/blogs/iso-7724/ (August 2026)
- **Relevance:** HIGH
- **Summary:** Detailed technical review of ISO 7724 (colorimetry for paints/varnishes) applied to portable spectrophotometers. Covers d/8° integrating sphere geometry, SCI/SCE modes, grating spectroscopy vs. filter-based technology, and D65/A illuminant standardization. The LISUN HSCD series portable spectrophotometers achieve laboratory-grade accuracy in portable formats.
- **Product Relevance:** Directly relevant to ColorGenius's Phase 2 spectrophotometer hardware strategy. ISO 7724-compliant portable spectrophotometers are now available and affordable. Key technical specs to evaluate for our hardware integration: d/8° geometry, 400-700nm range, D65 illuminant, Delta E*ab calculations.
- **Action Needed:** Research LISUN HSCD-780/800/860 specs and pricing as potential alternatives to the X-Rite/MetaVue devices previously evaluated. Compare against Acaia Pearl scale integration timeline.
