## Top Keywords & Content Themes

Based on the educators above, the most common educational content themes include:

1. **Balayage Technique** - Hand-painted highlights, freehand color placement
2. **Color Correction** - Fixing bad color, banding removal, color build-up issues
3. **Vivid/Fantasy Color** - Bright colors, pastels, color melting, creative applications
4. **Blonding** - Bleach application, toning, maintaining blonde hair health
5. **Color Theory** - Understanding underlying pigment, color wheel, formulation basics
6. **Celebrity Color** - Red carpet color, natural-looking dimension, glossy finishes
