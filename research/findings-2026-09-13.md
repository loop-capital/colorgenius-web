## Category C: FDA / Regulatory (September 2026)

### Finding 6: FDA Revokes Red No. 3 Authorization (Not Hair-Relevant)
- **Source:** https://www.nutritionaloutlook.com/view/fda-revokes-authorization-to-use-red-no-3-as-a-color-additive-in-food-or-drugs (September 3, 2026) — fetch blocked by Cloudflare, summary from search snippet
- **Relevance:** LOW
- **Summary:** FDA revoked authorization for Red No. 3 (erythrosine) as a food and drug color additive under the Delaney Clause. The additive was already banned in cosmetics since 1990, so this has no impact on hair color products. FDA maintains the additive is safe for humans but is legally obligated to revoke due to rat cancer studies.
- **Product Relevance:** No impact on hair color. Already banned in cosmetics.
