### Challenges Encountered
- Many brand artistic team pages are behind Cloudflare protection or have moved
- Redken's site (redkenpro.com) has aggressive bot detection
- Joico site blocks automated requests
- Matrix 404 on all artist page attempts
- Davines returns store locator instead of artistic team
- Goldwell requires region selection before showing content

### Recommendation
For Phase 1 beta at Pleij Salon, prioritize **Wella** and **Matrix** educators since these brands are most commonly used in US salons. **Tracey Cunningham** (Redken) and **George Papanikolas** (Matrix) would provide the highest social proof value.
