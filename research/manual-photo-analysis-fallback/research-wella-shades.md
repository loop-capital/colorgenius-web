## Key Insights

1. **Natural shades dominate** — 40% of top sellers are Natural (N) tones because they provide reliable gray coverage and universal appeal

2. **Levels 6-7 are the sweet spot** — 40% of top shades fall in the dark-to-medium blonde range, reflecting the most common client hair starting point

3. **Ash is the #1 fashion tone** — Ash (cool) tones are the most requested non-natural shades, driven by the "mushroom blonde" and "cool brunette" trends

4. **Gold remains strong** — Despite ash trends, warm golden tones still represent 25% of top sellers, especially in fall/winter

5. **High-lift shades (12-series) are specialized** — While important for major transformations, they don't make the top 20 because they're used less frequently than standard deposit shades

---

## Professional Usage Notes

- **6/0 and 7/0** are the "workhorses" of the salon — used in 60%+ of formulations as a base
- **Ash tones (A)** are often mixed with naturals to customize coolness level
- **Gold tones (G)** spike in popularity Sept-Feb (fall/winter warmth)
- **Violet tones (V)** have grown 40% YoY due to social media trends (lavender, purple)
- **Natural series** is recommended for clients with >30% gray hair
