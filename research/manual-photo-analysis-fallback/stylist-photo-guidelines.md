# ColorGenius — Stylist Photo Guidelines

## Purpose
This document provides professional guidelines for capturing client hair photos that produce the most accurate AI color analysis. Following these guidelines ensures reliable results and reduces the need for manual fallback.

---

## Equipment Requirements

### Minimum Requirements
- **Camera:** Smartphone with 12MP+ rear camera, or dedicated digital camera
- **Lighting:** Natural daylight or ring light / softbox
- **Background:** Solid, neutral-colored backdrop (white, gray, or black)

### Recommended Equipment
- **Camera:** iPhone 12+ or equivalent Android with portrait mode
- **Lighting:** 18-inch ring light with adjustable color temperature (5600K daylight)
- **Tripod:** Stable tripod with phone mount for consistent framing
- **Color Reference:** X-Rite ColorChecker Passport or gray card
- **Background:** Professional collapsible backdrop (white/gray)

---

## Photo Setup

### 1. Lighting

**DO:**
- Use **natural daylight** near a large window (north-facing preferred)
- Use a **ring light or softbox** positioned directly in front of client
- Ensure **even, diffused lighting** with no harsh shadows
- Set color temperature to **5600K (daylight)** if using artificial light
- Use **two lights** (key + fill) for best results

**DON'T:**
- Use fluorescent or mixed lighting (causes color casts)
- Shoot under direct overhead salon lights only
- Create shadows on the hair from the face or body
- Use flash directly on hair (causes overexposure and reflection)

### 2. Background

**DO:**
- Use a **solid, neutral background** (white, light gray, or black)
- Ensure background is **clean and uncluttered**
- Place client **2-3 feet from background** to avoid shadow spill

**DON'T:**
- Use busy, patterned, or colorful backgrounds
- Include salon equipment, mirrors, or other clients
- Use backgrounds that match the hair color (dark hair on black background)

### 3. Client Positioning

**DO:**
- Position client facing camera, **head slightly tilted down** (15-20°)
- Capture hair from **multiple angles**:
  - Front view (face-forward, hair over shoulders)
  - Side view (left and right)
  - Back view (if possible)
- Show **2-3 inches of root growth** (critical for accurate level analysis)
- Part hair to show **scalp area** for root analysis

**DON'T:**
- Shoot from below (unflattering, distorts hair appearance)
- Have client look up (hides roots)
- Capture only styled hair (must see natural texture)

### 4. Camera Settings

**DO:**
- Use **highest resolution** available
- Set to **auto-white balance** or manually calibrate with gray card
- Enable **grid lines** for level composition
- Focus on the **mid-lengths of hair**
- Keep ISO as **low as possible** (100-400)

**DON'T:**
- Use filters or portrait mode blur (obscures hair detail)
- Shoot in low resolution
- Use digital zoom (reduces quality)
- Overexpose or underexpose

## Required Photo Types

### 1. Current Hair Photo (Mandatory)
**Purpose:** Analyze existing color, condition, and texture

**Requirements:**
- Full hair visible, from roots to ends
- Natural or minimally styled (no heavy product)
- Dry hair (wet hair darkens and skews level detection)
- Include root area clearly
- At least 2 angles (front + side or back)

**File specs:** JPG/PNG, minimum 1024x1024px, under 20MB

### 2. Target/Inspiration Photo (Optional)
**Purpose:** Communicate desired result to AI

**Requirements:**
- Clear example of desired color
- Good quality lighting
- Similar hair texture/length preferred
- Can be from magazine, Pinterest, or previous client

### 3. Texture/Condition Close-Up (Optional but Recommended)
**Purpose:** Assess porosity, damage, and strand health

**Requirements:**
- Macro or close-up of individual strands
- Focus on ends for damage assessment
- Natural light
- Show cuticle condition if possible

## Hair Preparation Before Photo

### DO:
- Wash hair **24 hours before** photo (not same day)
- Remove all styling products
- Gently detangle
- Let hair air dry or blow dry straight
- Part hair in natural part

### DON'T:
- Apply oils, serums, or shine products before photo
- Style with heavy heat (changes texture appearance)
- Color hair within 48 hours of photo
- Take photos immediately after salon service

## Common Photo Problems & Solutions

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Color looks darker than real | Poor lighting, underexposed | Increase light, use white background |
| Color looks lighter than real | Overexposure, flash | Diffuse light, no direct flash |
| Roots not visible | Wrong angle, hair styled over | Tilt head down, part hair |
| Red/orange color cast | Tungsten or warm lighting | Use 5600K daylight bulbs |
| Green color cast | Fluorescent lighting | Use natural light or daylight LEDs |
| Blurry hair detail | Camera shake, low light | Use tripod, increase light |
| Face too prominent | Wrong framing | Zoom out, include full hair |

## Photo Checklist

Before submitting, verify:

- [ ] Lighting is even and daylight-balanced
- [ ] Background is solid and neutral
- [ ] Full hair is visible including roots
- [ ] Hair is dry and product-free
- [ ] Photo is in focus (especially mid-lengths)
- [ ] File is under 20MB
- [ ] At least 1024x1024 resolution
- [ ] No filters or effects applied
- [ ] Multiple angles captured (if possible)

## Troubleshooting

### AI Analysis Failed?

If the AI cannot analyze the photo:

1. **Check lighting** — Is it even and daylight-balanced?
2. **Check background** — Is it solid and neutral?
3. **Check focus** — Is hair detail sharp?
4. **Check roots** — Are they visible?
5. **Retake photo** following guidelines above
6. **Use manual fallback** if photo quality is still insufficient

### Unsure About Level?

Use this quick reference during manual fallback:

| Level | Look For |
|-------|----------|
| 1-2 | Black, no visible lightness |
| 3-4 | Dark brown, minimal warmth |
| 5-6 | Medium brown to dark blonde |
| 7-8 | Golden blonde, visible warmth |
| 9-10 | Pale blonde, platinum |

### Unsure About Undertone?

**Warm:** Hair looks golden, yellow, or orange in sunlight
**Cool:** Hair looks ashy, violet, or blue in sunlight
**Neutral:** No dominant warm or cool cast
