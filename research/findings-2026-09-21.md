### 1. Haut.AI Launches B2B AI Hair Analysis
- **Source:** https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hautai-launches-ai-powered-hair-analysis-for-beauty-brands-worldwide-302865372.html
- **Title:** "Haut.AI Launches AI-Powered Hair Analysis for Beauty Brands Worldwide"
- **Relevance:** HIGH
- **Summary:** Haut.AI (Estonia-based SaaS) launched commercial Hair Analysis on Sept 1, 2026. It uses a smartphone selfie + short questionnaire to evaluate 6 visible hair characteristics and recommend products from a brand's catalog. Expands their existing skin-analysis platform into hair. B2B SaaS model targeting beauty brands and retailers.
- **Product Relevance:** Direct competitor to ColorGenius's consultation/analysis features. Haut.AI has raised significant VC funding and already serves skincare brands — they have resources and distribution. Their approach is consumer-facing product recommendation, not professional formulation. Differentiator: ColorGenius targets salon professionals with actual formula generation, not just product matching.
- **Action Needed:** Monitor Haut.AI partnership announcements. Assess whether they expand into professional/formulation space. Consider whether ColorGenius could license their selfie-analysis tech rather than build from scratch.

### 4. Fall 2026 Hair Color Trends: Warm, Rich, Dimensional
- **Source:** https://www.vogue.com/article/hair-trends-2026 (Sept 2, 2026); https://www.cosmopolitan.com/style-beauty/beauty/a73643155/fall-hair-color-trends-2026/ (Sept 9, 2026); https://www.bustle.com/beauty/fall-2026-hair-color-trends (Sept 11, 2026)
- **Title:** "16 Hair Trends You're About to See Everywhere in 2026" (Vogue); "Fall 2026 Hair Color Trends" (Cosmo, Bustle)
- **Relevance:** MEDIUM
- **Summary:** Multiple industry publications confirm Fall 2026 trends: organic blonde (no obvious highlights/balayage lines), peach blonde, mulled wine (deep berry-brunette), maple cream blonde, spicy copper, popstar pink, burgundy. Movement away from overt highlights toward "lived-in" dimension and gloss. Warm tones dominate.
- **Product Relevance:** ColorGenius formulation engine should ensure strong coverage of these trending shades across all 21 brands. Marketing/content can reference these trends. The "organic blonde" trend (finely woven, delicate blend of tones) is technically demanding — an area where AI formulation can outperform manual mixing.
- **Action Needed:** Verify ColorGenius's current shade coverage includes these specific trend colors (peach blonde, mulled wine, maple cream). Consider adding seasonal trend filters to the formulation UI.
