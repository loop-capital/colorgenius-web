# Kenra — Kenra Color Permanent, Kenra Color Demi-Permanent, Simply Blonde

## Brand Overview
Kenra Professional offers several color lines:
- **Kenra Color Permanent**: Permanent crème color
- **Kenra Color Demi-Permanent**: Demi-permanent crème color
- **Simply Blonde**: High-lift toning and blonding system
- **Rapid Toner**: Fast-acting demi-permanent toner
- **Monochrome Collection**: Enhanced pigment permanent line for gray coverage

## Kenra Color Permanent

### Shade System
- Level: 1-12 (1=darkest black, 12=ultra lightest blonde)
- Tone: Letter-based system — first letter = dominant tone, second = secondary
- Format: Level + Tone Letter(s) (e.g., 7NA = level 7 with natural ash tone)

### Kenra Developer (Volumes)
- 10 vol (3%): Deposit only, no lift
- 20 vol (6%): Standard lift (1-2 levels)
- 30 vol (9%): High lift (2-3 levels)
- 40 vol (12%): Maximum lift (3-5 levels)

### Processing Times
- Standard processing: 30 minutes
- Resistant gray: 40 minutes
- Simply Blonde: 30-50 minutes

## Kenra Color Demi-Permanent

### Key Characteristics
- Demi-permanent crème color
- Low ammonia
- Lasts up to 24-28 shampoos
- Gray blending up to 50%
- Mixing ratio: 1:1 with Kenra 10 Volume Developer
- Processing time: 10-20 minutes

## Simply Blonde

### Key Characteristics
- High-lift permanent toning system
- Up to 5 levels of lift on natural hair
- Mixing ratio: 1:2 (color:developer) with 40 vol
- Processing time: 30-50 minutes
- Available in: Pearl, Ice, Cool, Natural, Extra Cool

## Rapid Toner

### Key Characteristics
- Fast-acting demi-permanent toner
- Processing time: 5 minutes
- Mixing ratio: 1:2 (toner:developer) with 9 vol
- Neutralizes unwanted warmth quickly
- Available in: Pearl, Ice, Natural, Violet
