# Redken — Shades EQ, Color Extend, Blondage

## Brand Overview
Redken Professional offers several color lines:
- **Shades EQ**: Acidic demi-permanent color for toning, refreshing, and glossing
- **Color Extend**: Permanent color for gray coverage and vibrancy
- **Blondage**: Specialized blonding and toning line

## Redken Shades EQ (Acidic Demi-Permanent)

### Shade System
- Level: 1-10 (1=darkest black, 10=lightest blonde)
- Tone: Letter-based system indicating undertones
- Format: Level + Tone Letters (e.g., 5RR = level 5 with red-red tone)

### Tone Letter Meanings
- **N**: Neutral (neither cool nor warm)
- **V**: Violet (cool, neutralizes yellow/brassy tones)
- **G**: Gold (warm, adds richness and warmth)
- **B**: Blue (cool, neutralizes orange tones)
- **A**: Ash (cool, neutralizes red/orange tones)
- **C**: Copper (warm, reddish-brown tones)
- **R**: Red (warm, vibrant red tones)
- **T**: Titanium (cool, smoky, ashy tones)
- **M**: Mahogany (warm, reddish-brown base)
- **RB**: Red-Blue (violet-red, cool reddish tones)
- **RV**: Red-Violet (burgundy tones)
- **RG**: Red-Gold (strawberry blonde tones)
- **NA**: Neutral Ash (cool neutral with ash undertone)
- **NB**: Natural Brown (warm neutral with brown base)

### Key Characteristics
- Gentle acidic demi-permanent color
- Ammonia-free formula
- Lasts up to 28 shampoos
- Excellent for toning, refreshing, and glossing
- Can be used for gray blending (up to 50%)
- Mixing ratio: 1:1 with Redken Shades EQ Processing Solution
- Processing time: 10-20 minutes
- pH between 4.5-5.5 for cuticle sealing and shine
