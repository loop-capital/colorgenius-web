# Goldwell — Topchic, Colorance, Elumen

## Brand Overview
Goldwell offers several color lines:
- **Topchic**: Permanent oxidative color
- **Colorance**: Demi-permanent acid color
- **Elumen**: Non-oxidative high-performance direct dye
- **Topchic Beige Blonde**: Specialized beige blonde series
- **Natural Colorance**: Enhanced natural series for gray coverage

## Goldwell Topchic (Permanent)

### Shade System
- Level: 1-12 (1=darkest black, 12=ultra lightest blonde)
- Tone: Letter-based system — first letter = dominant tone, second = secondary
- Format: Level + Tone Letter(s) (e.g., 7NA = Level 7 Natural Ash)
- Double letters (KK, RR) indicate extra strong/intense tones

### NA Shades — Key Feature
- **NA = Natural Ash**: Designed for fine to normal hair requiring cool color direction
- Calibrated for cool results while maintaining gray coverage
- Not as intensely cool as pure A (Ash) shades

### Key Characteristics
- Up to 100% gray coverage
- Up to 5 levels of lift
- Mixing ratio: 1:1 with Topchic Developer (Oxytocin)
- Processing time: 30-40 minutes
- Topchic shades enriched with multidimensional Elumen reflects

### Topchic Developer (Oxytocin) (Volumes)
- 5 vol (1.5%): Deposit only, no lift
- 10 vol (3%): Deposit with slight lift
- 20 vol (6%): Standard lift (1-2 levels)
- 30 vol (9%): High lift (2-3 levels)
- 40 vol (12%): Maximum lift (3-5 levels)

### Processing Times
- Standard processing: 30-40 minutes
- Gray coverage: 35-45 minutes
- High-lift: 40-50 minutes

## Goldwell Colorance (Demi-Permanent)

### Shade System
- Same letter-based tone system as Topchic
- Level: 1-12

### Key Characteristics
- Acid demi-permanent color
- No ammonia formula
- Lasts up to 24-28 shampoos
- Gray blending up to 50%
- Mixing ratio: 1:1 with Colorance Developer (emulsion)
- Processing time: 10-25 minutes
- Gentle on hair, adds incredible shine

### Colorance Developer Options
- 6 vol (2%): Gentle deposit only
- 10 vol (3%): Standard demi processing
- 15 vol (4.5%): Enhanced demi with slight lift

## Goldwell Elumen (Non-Oxidative)

### Key Characteristics
- Non-oxidative high-performance direct dye
- No developer required
- Pure color molecule penetration technology
- Lasts 20+ shampoos
- 20+ intermixable shades
- Processing time: 20-30 minutes under heat
- Extraordinary shine and durability

### Elumen Shade Categories
| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Prepare | Pre-pigmenting shades for even results |
| Play | Fashion forward vivid shades |
| Perform | High-performance natural tones |
| Pure | Clear and correction shades |
