## 3. Color Melting / Root Shadow

**What it is:** Seamless blend of 2-3 shades from root to ends with no demarcation. Root zone is deepest, ends are lightest.

**Key Technique:** Apply root shade first, overlap mid-shade, then ends — process all together for last 5-10 minutes.

## Pro Tips from Working Colorists

1. **"The root shadow is your best friend"** — saves you from harsh grow-out lines

2. **"Always pull the root shade down slightly before applying mids"** — creates the "melt"

3. **"Use Clear (0N) with end formulas"** — prevents over-deposit on porous ends

4. **"Process root smudges 5 min alone first"** — then pull through for last 5-10 min

5. **"Herringbone is the future of gray coverage"** — clients are tired of solid color

6. **"Expensive blonde is really just 'no shortcuts' blonde"** — full foiling, no balayage shortcuts

7. **"The gloss is the secret sauce"** — every service should end with a gloss
