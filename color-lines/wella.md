# Wella — Koleston Perfect, Illumina, Color Touch

## Brand Overview
Wella Professionals offers several color lines:
- **Koleston Perfect**: Permanent color with superior gray coverage
- **Illumina**: Permanent color with light technology for multidimensional results
- **Color Touch**: Demi-permanent color for tone enhancement and shine

## Wella Koleston Perfect (Permanent)

### Shade System
- Depth: 1-10 (1=darkest black, 10=lightest blonde)
- Tone: 0=natural, 1=ash, 2=cool ash/green, 3=gold, 4=red, 5=mahogany, 6=violet, 7=brunette, 8=pearl/blue, 9=cendre/grey
- Format: Depth/Tone (e.g., 5/3 = level 5 with gold tone)

### Key Characteristics
- Superior gray coverage (up to 100%)
- ME+ (MEA) technology for reduced allergy risk
- Idye technology for intense, long-lasting color
- Available in 102 intermixable shades
- Mixing ratio: 1:1 with Welloxon Perfect developer
- Processing time: 30-40 minutes

### Welloxon Perfect Developer (Volumes)
- Welloxon Perfect 1.9% (6 vol): Deposit only, no lift
- Welloxon Perfect 3% (10 vol): Deposit with slight lift (1-2 levels)
- Welloxon Perfect 6% (20 vol): Standard lift (2-3 levels)
- Welloxon Perfect 9% (30 vol): High lift (3-4 levels)
- Welloxon Perfect 12% (40 vol): Maximum lift (4-5 levels)

### Processing Times
- Gray coverage: 30-40 minutes
- Standard processing: 30-40 minutes
- Maximum lift: 40 minutes
- Toning/demi-permanent: 10-20 minutes
