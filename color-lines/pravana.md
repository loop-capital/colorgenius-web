# Pravana — ChromaSilk, ChromaSilk VIVIDS, ChromaSilk Platinum Toner

## Brand Overview
Pravana offers several color lines:
- **ChromaSilk**: Permanent crème color with silk & proteins
- **ChromaSilk VIVIDS**: Semi-permanent, non-oxidative direct dyes
- **ChromaSilk Platinum Toner**: Demi-permanent toning system
- **ChromaSilk Ultra Hi-Lifts**: High-lift permanent blonding

## Pravana ChromaSilk (Permanent)

### Shade System
- Level: 1-10+ (1=darkest black, 10+=ultra light blonde)
- Tone: Decimal system — first number after decimal = ~70% of tonal base, second number = ~30%
- Format: Level.Tone (e.g., 7.43 = Level 7, 70% Copper + 30% Gold)
- Also features American-Style alpha-numeric system on packaging for convenience

### Tone Number Meanings
| Number | Name | Base Tone | Neutralizes |
|--------|------|-----------|-------------|
| **.0** | Natural | Slight blue | Warmth (ideal for gray coverage) |
| **.1** | Ash | Cool green | Red undertones |
| **.2** | Beige | Blue-violet | Orange undertones |
| **.3** | Gold | Cool gold | Violet/blue undertones |
| **.4** | Copper | Orange | Blue/violet undertones |
| **.5** | Mahogany | Violet-red | Green undertones |
| **.6** | Red | Red | Green undertones |
| **.7** | Violet | Violet | Yellow/gold undertones |
| **.8** | Pearl | Soft pink + cool silver | Yellow/warmth |

### Double Tone Notation
- When same tone appears twice (e.g., .11 = Double Ash), it indicates double intensity
- **.11**: Double Ash (double green base — cancels red, leaves ash reflections)
- **.00**: Double Natural (intense gray coverage)

### Key Characteristics
- Silk and protein-infused formula
- Up to 100% gray coverage
- Up to 4-5 levels of lift
- 84 intermixable shades (permanent + demi-permanent)
- Mixing ratio: 1:1 with Pravana Developer
- Processing time: 30-40 minutes
- Low ammonia formula

### ChromaSilk Color Correctors
- **CC-Blue**: Neutralizes orange/brass
- **CC-Green**: Neutralizes red
- **CC-Violet**: Neutralizes yellow
- **CC-Red**: Adds red warmth
- **CC-Yellow/Gold**: Adds gold warmth
- **CC-Copper**: Adds copper warmth
- Use up to 1/4 oz per 2 oz total formula

### Pravana Developer (Volumes)
- 10 vol (3%): Deposit only, no lift
- 20 vol (6%): Standard lift (1-2 levels)
- 30 vol (9%): High lift (2-3 levels)
- 40 vol (12%): Maximum lift (3-5 levels)

### Processing Times
- Standard processing: 30-40 minutes
- Gray coverage: 35-45 minutes
- High-lift: 40-50 minutes
