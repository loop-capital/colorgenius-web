# Joico — LumiShine, Vero K-PAK Color, Age Defy

## Brand Overview
Joico offers several color lines:
- **LumiShine**: Permanent crème color with shine technology
- **Vero K-PAK Color**: Permanent color with Quadramine Complex for repair
- **LumiShine Repair+**: Demi-permanent liquid color
- **Age Defy**: Anti-aging color for mature hair

## Joico LumiShine (Permanent Crème)

### Shade System
- Level: 1-10 (1=darkest black, 10=lightest blonde)
- Tone: Letter-based system — First Letter = Dominant Tone, Second Letter = Secondary Tone
- Also uses Global Tonal Number Key System (e.g., .0, .01, .03, .04, etc.)

- LumiShine Crème technology for high-shine results

### LumiShine Developer (Volumes)
- 5 vol (1.5%): Deposit only, no lift
- 10 vol (3%): Deposit with slight lift (1 level)
- 20 vol (6%): Standard lift (1-2 levels)
- 30 vol (9%): High lift (2-3 levels)
- 40 vol (12%): Maximum lift (3-5 levels)

### Processing Times
- Gray coverage: 35-45 minutes
- Standard processing: 30-40 minutes
- Maximum lift: 40-45 minutes
