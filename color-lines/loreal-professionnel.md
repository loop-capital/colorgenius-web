# L'Oréal Professionnel — iNOA, Majirel, Dia Light, Dia Colour

## Brand Overview
L'Oréal Professionnel offers several color lines:
- **iNOA** (Innovation No Ammonia): Ammonia-free permanent color with Oil Delivery System (ODS²)
- **Majirel**: Permanent high-lift color with Incell technology
- **Dia Light**: Ammonia-free demi-permanent acidic gloss
- **Dia Colour**: Demi-permanent alkaline color
- **Blond Studio**: Lightening and blonding system

## L'Oréal Professionnel iNOA (Permanent — No Ammonia)

### Shade System
- Level: 1-10+ (1=darkest black, 10+=ultra light blonde)
- Tone: Decimal system — first digit after decimal = ~70% dominant tone, second digit = ~30% secondary tone
- Format: Level.ToneTone (e.g., 7.43 = Level 7, 70% Copper + 30% Gold)
- Also uses letter abbreviations on some packaging

### Tone Number Meanings
| Number | Name | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **.0** | Natural | Neutral base |
| **.1** | Ash / Cendré | Cool blue-green based |
| **.2** | Green / Irises | Cool green-based |
| **.3** | Gold | Warm yellow-gold |
| **.4** | Copper / Mahogany | Warm orange-copper |
| **.5** | Mahogany / Red | Warm violet-red |
| **.6** | Red | Warm red |
| **.7** | Violet / Mat | Cool violet-matte |
| **.8** | Pearl / Blue | Cool blue-pearl |
| **.9** | Cendré / Grey | Cool grey |

### Key Characteristics
- Oil Delivery System (ODS²) — ammonia-free technology
- Up to 100% gray coverage without ammonia
- Up to 3 levels of lift
- Mixing ratio: 1:1 with iNOA Oxydant
- Processing time: 35-40 minutes
- 6% maximum developer for on-scalp use
- Comfortable client experience (no ammonia odor)

### iNOA Developer (Oxydant) (Volumes)
- 6 vol (1.8%): Slight deposit
- 10 vol (3%): Deposit with minimal lift
- 20 vol (6%): Standard lift (1-2 levels)
- 30 vol (9%): High lift (2-3 levels) — max for on-scalp

### Processing Times
- Standard processing: 35-40 minutes
- Gray coverage: 40 minutes
- High-lift: 40-50 minutes

## L'Oréal Professionnel Majirel (Permanent)

### Shade System
- Same decimal tone system as iNOA
- Level: 1-10+

### Key Characteristics
- Permanent color with Incell technology
- Up to 100% gray coverage
- Up to 5 levels of lift
- Mixing ratio: 1:1.5 with Majirel Oxydant
- Processing time: 35 minutes
- Ionène G protective coating technology
- Available in High-Lift series (Majirel High-Lift)

### Majirel High-Lift
- Specialized high-lift permanent series
- Up to 5 levels of lift
- Available in cool and warm tone directions
- Mixing ratio: 1:2 with 40 vol Oxydant
- Processing time: 35-50 minutes

## L'Oréal Professionnel Dia Light (Demi-Permanent — Acidic)

### Shade System
- Same decimal tone system
- Level: 1-10

### Key Characteristics
- Ammonia-free acidic demi-permanent gloss
- Acidic technology for gentle processing
- Lasts up to 24-28 shampoos
- Gray blending up to 50%
- Mixing ratio: 1:1.5 with Dia Light Oxydant (9 vol)
- Processing time: 10-20 minutes
- Enhances shine and adds tonal depth
- Perfect for glossing, toning, and color refreshing

## L'Oréal Professionnel Dia Colour (Demi-Permanent — Alkaline)

### Key Characteristics
- Demi-permanent alkaline color
- Lasts up to 24-28 shampoos
- Up to 2 levels of lift
- Gray blending up to 70%
- Mixing ratio: 1:1.5 with Dia Colour Oxydant (15 vol)
- Processing time: 15-25 minutes
- More lift potential than Dia Light

## Notes
- All information based on publicly available professional resources and educational materials
- L'Oréal Professionnel decimal system is nearly identical to Davines (same parent company influence)
- Key difference: .8 = Pearl/Blue in L'Oréal vs .2 = Green/Iridescent in Davines
- iNOA = Innovation No Ammonia — the flagship ammonia-free permanent line
- Majirel = Traditional ammonia permanent with Incell + Ionène G
- Dia Light = Acidic demi (no ammonia) — for gloss/toning
- Dia Colour = Alkaline demi (low ammonia) — for more deposit/lift
- Mixing ratios: iNOA/Majirel 1:1 to 1:1.5; Dia 1:1.5
- Processing times may vary based on desired result and developer volume
- Always consult manufacturer instructions for exact specifications
- This documentation is for ColorGenius AI formulation engine development
