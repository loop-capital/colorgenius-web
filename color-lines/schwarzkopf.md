# Schwarzkopf — Igora Royal, Igora Vibrance, Igora Vario

## Brand Overview
Schwarzkopf Professional offers several color lines:
- **Igora Royal**: Permanent color with true-to-swatch results
- **Igora Vibrance**: Demi-permanent color for tone-on-tone coloring
- **Igora Vario**: Lightening and bleaching products

## Schwarzkopf Igora Royal (Permanent)

### Shade System
- Level: 1-9 (1=darkest black, 9=extra light blonde, 10=special toner level)
- Tone: Number-based system after dash indicating tone direction
- Format: Level-ToneDirection (e.g., 5-5 = level 5 with gold tone)

### Tone Direction Meanings
- **-0**: Natural (neutral)
- **-1**: Cendré (blue/violet - grey ash)
- **-2**: Ash (blue ash)
- **-3**: Matte (green ash)
- **-4**: Beige (muted gold)
- **-5**: Gold (yellow)
- **-6**: Chocolate (warm brown - red, blue and yellow)
- **-7**: Copper (orange)
- **-8**: Red (red)
- **-9**: Violet (purple)

### Key Characteristics
- True-to-swatch color results
- Ammonia-free options available
- Up to 100% gray coverage
- Igora Royal Intense: Ultra-vivid fashion shades
- Mixing ratio: 1:1 with Igora Royal Developer (various volumes)
- Processing time: 30-40 minutes
- Vibrant, long-lasting color

### Igora Royal Developer (Volumes)
- Igora Royal Developer 1.9% (6 vol): Deposit only, no lift
- Igora Royal Developer 3% (10 vol): Deposit with slight lift (1-2 levels)
- Igora Royal Developer 6% (20 vol): Standard lift (2-3 levels)
- Igora Royal Developer 9% (30 vol): High lift (3-4 levels)
- Igora Royal Developer 12% (40 vol): Maximum lift (up to 5 levels with Igora Royal)

### Processing Times
- Gray coverage: 30-40 minutes
- Standard processing: 30-40 minutes
- Maximum lift: 40 minutes
- Toning/demi-permanent: 10-20 minutes

## Schwarzkopf Igora Vibrance (Demi-Permanent)

### Shade System
- Same numbering system as Igora Royal: Level-ToneDirection
- Specifically formulated for tone-on-tone coloring and gray blending

### Key Characteristics
- Demi-permanent (lasts up to 25 washes for natural shades)
- Excellent for gray blending (up to 50%)
- Low ammonia formula
- High shine and conditioning
- Mixing ratio: 1:1 with Igora Vibrance Developer (lotion or gel)
- Processing time: 10-20 minutes
- Pastel shades and boosters: up to 12 washes
