# Davines — View, A New Colour, Mask with Vibrachrom

## Brand Overview
Davines offers three main color lines:
- **View**: Tone-on-tone color line with acidic pH for shine and fullness
- **A New Colour**: Ammonia-free permanent color system for total gray coverage
- **Mask with Vibrachrom**: Permanent cream-based color with Vibrachrom™ technology for intense conditioning

## Davines View (Tone-on-Tone)

### Shade System
- Level: 1-10 (1=darkest black, 10=lightest blonde)
- Tone: .1=ash, .2=green, .3=gold, .4=copper, .5=mahogany, .6=red, .7=mat/green, .8=mocha/brown, .9=soft ash blue

### Key Characteristics
- Acidic pH for shine and fullness
- Tone-on-tone coloring (adds tone without significant level change)
- Can disguise up to 50% white hair
- Mixing ratio: 1 part color + 1 part Activator 10 vol
- Processing time: 5-20 minutes (visual control)
