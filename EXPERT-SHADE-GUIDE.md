## MEDICAL CONDITION PROTOCOLS

### Conditions Affecting Color

| Condition | Consideration | Developer/Formula Adjustment |
|-----------|---------------|------------------------------|
| **Thyroid** | Processes faster; unpredictable uptake | **5-10vol only** |
| **Menopause** | Texture/porosity changes | **5-10vol only** |
| **Cancer treatment** | Wait **1 year** before color; extremely fragile | Avoid or ammonia-free |
| **Alopecia** | Scalp sensitivity; patch test essential | **Ammonia-free only** |
| **Psoriasis** | Scalp inflammation; avoid harsh chemicals | **Ammonia-free only** |
