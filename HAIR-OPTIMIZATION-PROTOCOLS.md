## MEDICATION-SPECIFIC OPTIMIZATION

### For Clients on Photosensitizing Medications

**Goal:** Minimize photo-reactivity, support color stability

**Recommendations:**
1. **Metal Detox** before every color service (mandatory)
2. **Antioxidant support** — Vitamin C, E, selenium
3. **UV protection** — Wear hats, use UV-protectant hair products
4. **Timing** — Schedule color services away from peak sun exposure periods

**Expected Outcome:** Reduced unpredictability, not elimination of medication effects

### For Post-Chemo Clients

**Goal:** Accelerate safe regrowth, optimize first color service timing

**Recommendations:**
1. **Collagen + amino acids** — structural support
2. **Scalp massage + gentle exfoliation** — stimulate follicles
3. **Low laser light therapy** — cellular stimulation
4. **Gentle, sulfate-free care** — don't stress fragile new hair
5. **Patience** — minimum 6 months before color, texture may differ permanently

### For Thyroid Clients

**Goal:** Support TSH stability, optimize hair quality

**Recommendations:**
1. **Selenium** — supports thyroid function (Brazil nuts, supplement)
2. **Zinc** — thyroid hormone synthesis
3. **Wait for TSH stability** — 4-6 weeks after medication adjustment
4. **Lower developer volume** — 5-10vol regardless of optimization

## PROTOCOL SUMMARY

### For Best Color Outcomes:

**4-6 Weeks Before:**
- Start collagen + amino acid supplementation
- Implement scalp massage routine
- Add shower filter
- Optimize diet (protein, omega-3, nutrients)

**1-2 Weeks Before:**
- Increase sauna usage (if appropriate)
- Focus on hydration

- L'Oréal Metal Detox pre-treatment

- Well-rested, low stress

**Ongoing:**
- Consistent supplementation
- Gentle hair care
- UV protection
- Regular scalp massage
