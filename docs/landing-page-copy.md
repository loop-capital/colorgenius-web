## How It Works

### Step 1: 📸 Capture Your Client's Hair
Take a photo of your client's hair (or upload one). Our AI reads the level, tone, and condition — accounting for previous treatments, texture, and safety flags like metallic dye or henna.

### Step 2: 🎨 Choose the Target Shade
Pick the shade your client wants from your preferred brand's catalog. ColorGenius cross-references starting level, underlying pigment, and desired outcome to calculate the exact formulation.

### Step 3: ✅ Get Your Formula — and Score the Result
Receive a complete formula card with mixing ratios, developer volume, and processing time. After the service, snap an after photo and our AI scores the result — learning and improving with every use.

## Feature Highlights

### 🧠 AI Photo Analysis
**See what the eye sometimes misses.**
Our AI detects hair level, tone, porosity, and condition from a single photo. It flags safety concerns — metallic dye, henna, previous chemical treatments — before they become problems at the bowl.

### 🎯 Precision Formulation
**The right formula, every time.**
No more flipping through shade charts or relying on memory. ColorGenius calculates the exact shade mix, developer volume, and processing time based on 10+ variables — including your client's unique hair history and the specific color line you're using.

### 📊 Result Scoring & Learning
**Every formulation makes the next one better.**
Our AI compares before and after photos, scoring color accuracy, hair condition, and application evenness. Over time, the engine learns from real-world results — your results — to deliver increasingly precise recommendations.

### 🔒 Client History & Safety
**Never start from scratch.**
Every client's questionnaire, photo, formulation, and result is saved to their profile. Returning clients? Their complete color history is one tap away — including safety flags, previous services, and what worked (or didn't).

### 📋 Smart Questionnaire
**Catch what matters before it's too late.**
Our pre-formulation questionnaire covers treatment history, allergies, texture, previous services, and desired outcomes. The AI uses every answer to refine its recommendation — and flags anything that could cause a reaction or unexpected result.

### 🏪 Multi-Line Support
**Your brand. Your way.**
ColorGenius supports the color lines you actually use — Redken, Wella, Schwarzkopf, Davines, L'Oréal Professionnel at launch, with more added based on demand. Formulations respect each brand's unique mixing ratios, developer systems, and processing times.

## FAQ Section

### General

**What is ColorGenius?**
ColorGenius is an AI-powered formulation platform built for professional hair colorists. It analyzes your client's hair through a photo, combines it with their treatment history and desired outcome, and delivers a precise formula in seconds.

**Is ColorGenius only for new stylists?**
No. ColorGenius helps stylists at every level — from recent graduates building confidence to veterans handling complex corrective color. The AI adapts to your experience.

**Does it replace my color training?**
ColorGenius is a tool, not a teacher. It handles the calculation so you can focus on the art. Think of it as a calculator for colorists — you still make the creative and professional decisions.

### Color Lines & Compatibility

**Which color lines do you support?**
Redken, Wella, Schwarzkopf, Davines, and L'Oréal Professionnel at launch, with more lines added based on demand.

**What if my color line isn't supported?**
Tell us! Email beta@colorgenius.ai with your preferred brand. We prioritize new line integrations based on demand and can typically add a line within 2-4 weeks during beta.

**Does ColorGenius use each brand's actual mixing ratios and developer systems?**
Yes. Formulations respect each brand's specific mixing ratios (e.g., 1:1 for Redken Shades EQ, 1:2 for Wella Color Touch), developer volumes, and processing times.

### Photo Analysis

**How does the AI analyze hair from a photo?**
Our AI uses computer vision to detect hair level (1-12), tone (warm, cool, neutral), porosity, and overall condition. It accounts for lighting conditions and flags when a photo quality issue might affect accuracy.

**How accurate is the photo analysis?**
90%+ accuracy on well-lit, properly taken photos. Best results come from natural daylight near a window. Harsh salon overhead lighting, flash, and wet hair can reduce accuracy.

**What if the AI reads my client's hair wrong?**
You can manually adjust any component of the formulation. Every adjustment teaches the AI to improve. Also, use the "Report Issue" button so we can investigate.

### Privacy & Data

**Is client data secure?**
Yes. All photos and client data are encrypted and never shared. We use anonymized formulation data to improve the AI — no personal information leaves our servers.

**Who can see my formulations?**
Only you. Your formulations are private to your account. In the future, you'll have the option to share to a community feed, but that's opt-in only.

**Do you sell data to manufacturers?**
No. We may share aggregated, anonymized usage trends with brand partners (e.g., "Shade 09V usage increased 15% this quarter") — but no individual stylist or client data is ever shared.

### Beta Program

**How much does beta cost?**
Completely free. Unlimited formulations, all supported color lines, full features. Beta testers get a lifetime discount on future paid plans as a thank-you.

**How many beta spots are available?**
We're limiting beta to 50 stylists to ensure quality support and rapid iteration. Additional cohorts will open based on demand.

**What's expected of me as a beta tester?**
Use ColorGenius for at least 3 formulations per week, complete a short weekly survey, and report any safety concerns immediately. That's it.

**What happens after beta?**
Beta testers transition to a paid plan with a permanent discount. We'll give you at least 30 days' notice before any transition, and your data stays intact.

### Technical

**What devices work with ColorGenius?**
Any modern browser — iPhone, iPad, Android, or desktop. Camera integration works on all devices with a browser camera. We recommend a tablet for salon use.

**Do I need to download an app?**
No. ColorGenius is a web app — just open it in your browser. We may offer native apps in the future, but the web version works everywhere.

**What if I don't have good internet at my salon?**
ColorGenius requires an internet connection for AI analysis. If your salon has spotty Wi-Fi, we recommend taking photos and running formulations when connectivity is stable. Offline mode is on our roadmap.
