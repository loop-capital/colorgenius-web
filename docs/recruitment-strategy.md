## Feedback Collection Plan

### In-App (Continuous)
| Mechanism | When | What It Captures |
|-----------|------|-----------------|
| **Thumbs up/down** on every formula | After each formulation | Quick sentiment — was this helpful? |
| **"Report Issue" button** | Any screen | Bug reports, wrong recommendations, UX friction |
| **Formula adjustment tracking** | When stylist overrides AI | What changed + why (optional free text) |
| **After-photo scoring** | After each scored result | Accuracy, condition, evenness scores |

### Weekly Survey (Monday delivery, 5 min)
**Delivered via:** Google Form link in email (transition to in-app survey post-beta)
**Questions:**

1. How many formulations did you complete this week? (0, 1-3, 4-7, 8+)
2. Overall, how accurate were the AI's recommendations? (1-5 scale)
3. Did you encounter any formula recommendations that were significantly off? (Yes/No — if yes, details)
4. Were any safety warnings helpful or missing? (Free text)
5. What's the one thing that would make ColorGenius more useful to you? (Free text)
6. Any bugs or issues to report? (Free text)
7. Would you recommend ColorGenius to another stylist? (1-10 NPS)

### Bi-Weekly 1:1 Check-ins (Phase 1 only)
- 15-minute video call with each of the first 10 stylists
- Walk through their formulations, ask about friction points
- Capture qualitative insights that surveys miss
- Record notes in memory/beta-feedback/

### Monthly Beta Report (Internal)
- Aggregate all feedback into a monthly report
- Categorize: bugs, feature requests, accuracy issues, UX friction
- Prioritize with dev team for next sprint
- Share highlights with beta group (shows we're listening)

### Feedback Storage
- All feedback saved to: `memory/beta-feedback/`
- Weekly survey results: `memory/beta-feedback/weekly-surveys/`
- 1:1 notes: `memory/beta-feedback/one-on-ones/`
- Monthly reports: `memory/beta-feedback/monthly-reports/`
