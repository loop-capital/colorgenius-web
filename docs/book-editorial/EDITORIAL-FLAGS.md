# COLORgenius Book — Editorial Red Flags
**Quick-scan findings for editorial priority review**

---

## 🔴 High Priority

### 1. Competitive Claims (Chapter 11)
Chapter 11 names specific companies with detailed critiques:
- **Schwarzkopf** — MAiA, SalonLab, House of Color, Hair Expert apps; detailed functional criticism
- **L'Oréal / ModiFace** — References $300M acquisition price; criticizes AR as "visualizer not formulator"
- **Matrix** — Detailed critique of 3D Virtual Hair Color Try-On limitations
- **Redken** — AR try-on limitations described
- **Vish** — Specific pricing ($299/mo), waste reduction claims (~30%), Bluetooth scale costs ($150-300)
- **eSalon, Color&Co** — Consumer-facing model critiques

**Action needed:** Legal review for defensibility. All factual claims (pricing, acquisition amounts, feature descriptions) should be verified. Consider adding a disclaimer or softening language.

### 2. No Legal Disclaimers Anywhere
Zero trademark symbols (™/®/©) on brand names. No disclaimers on formulas. No "results may vary" language. No "not affiliated with [brand]" statements.

**Action needed:** Add standard disclaimers:
- Brand name trademark attributions
- Formula disclaimer ("formulas shown are examples; always strand test")
- AI capability disclaimer ("COLORgenius assists formulation; final decisions rest with the colorist")

### 3. Specific Formulas Published
Chapters 5 and 7 contain specific brand shade combinations with developer volumes and timing. While appropriate for a professional audience, these should carry disclaimers.

---

## 🟡 Medium Priority

### 4. Absolute Language
Several statements use absolute language that could be problematic:
- "No permanent color in the world can do this in one step"
- "No product on earth"
- "The only guaranteed safe path"
- References to "100% grey" coverage strategies

**Action needed:** Soften to "most professional colors" / "no product I've worked with" or add qualifying language.

### 5. Competitor Pricing/Performance Claims
- Vish at $299/month — verify current pricing
- "30% waste reduction" attributed to Vish — source?
- ModiFace "nearly one billion consumers" — verify
- L'Oréal $300M acquisition — "reported" qualifier present but should verify

### 6. Chapter Length Imbalance
- **Ch 5 (Correction):** 6,066 words — longest chapter, but justified by 8 case studies
- **Ch 9 (Advanced Techniques):** 2,756 words — shortest substantive chapter; may feel thin compared to Ch 5
- **Ch 17 (Future):** 2,437 words — intentionally brief as closing chapter

**Action needed:** Editorial judgment on whether Ch 9 needs expansion.

---

## 🟢 Low Priority

### 7. Voice Consistency
Quick scan shows strong, consistent first-person "Tiche" voice throughout. No detected drops into third-person or marketing copy. This is a strength.

### 8. Terminology
Uses "colorist" consistently (not "stylist"). "Formula" and "formulation" used interchangeably — editorial may want to standardize.

### 9. Developer Terminology
Mixes "20-volume" and could reference "6%" — editorial should ensure consistency within chapters.

---

## Summary

The manuscript is technically strong and the voice is compelling. The primary risks are:
1. **Legal exposure** from competitive claims in Ch 11
2. **Missing disclaimers** across all chapters
3. **Absolute language** that could overpromise

These are all addressable in an editorial pass without changing the book's character.
