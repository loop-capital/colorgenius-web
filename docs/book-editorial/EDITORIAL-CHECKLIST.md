# COLORgenius Book — Editorial Checklist

Use this checklist during the review pass. Mark items as you go.

## Per-Chapter Checklist (copy for each chapter)

- [ ] Opening anecdote lands and hooks the reader
- [ ] Voice is consistently "Tiche" (first-person master colorist)
- [ ] Technical content is accurate and accessible
- [ ] No jargon without explanation (first use)
- [ ] Brand/formula references verified (Wella/Goldwell/Redken)
- [ ] No overpromising on AI capabilities
- [ ] Transitions to next chapter feel natural
- [ ] Grammar and punctuation clean
- [ ] No repeated crutch phrases
- [ ] Chapter length appropriate for topic depth

## Global Checklist

- [ ] Overall arc reads well (science → applied → industry → product → business → vision)
- [ ] Tone consistency across all 17 chapters
- [ ] Terminology consistent (e.g., "colorist" vs "stylist", "formula" vs "formulation")
- [ ] Brand name capitalization and ™/® usage consistent
- [ ] Developer volume terminology consistent (10/20/30/40 vol vs 3%/6%/9%/12%)
- [ ] Competitive mentions (Ch 11) are fair and defensible
- [ ] Business math (Ch 14, 16) is accurate
- [ ] No copyright/trademark issues with referenced products
- [ ] AI claims (Ch 12, 13) match actual product capabilities
- [ ] Legal disclaimers present where needed (formulas, brand references)

## Suggested Edits Format

Use inline comments with:
- **[VOICE]** — Voice/persona inconsistency
- **[FACT]** — Factual claim to verify
- **[BRAND]** — Brand/formula accuracy concern
- **[STRUCTURE]** — Structural/pacing suggestion
- **[COPY]** — Grammar/punctuation/style
- **[LEGAL]** — Potential liability or trademark issue
