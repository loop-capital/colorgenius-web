## Your First Formulation — Step by Step

### 3a. Create a New Client

1. From the **Dashboard**, tap **"New Formulation"**
2. Fill in the **Client Questionnaire**:

| Question | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| Treatment history (straightening, perm, acid) | Chemical treatments change how hair accepts color — affects developer choice and processing |
| Metallic dye or henna | **Critical safety flag** — can cause severe reactions with some color lines |
| Known allergies | **Critical safety flag** — formulation must avoid allergens |
| Hair texture (fine/medium/coarse) | Affects processing time and developer volume |
| Scalp condition | Sensitive scalps need gentler developer and shorter processing |
| Previous color services | Determines underlying pigment and how much lift is possible |
| Desired outcome (touch-up, full, highlights, corrective) | Changes the entire formulation approach |
| Hair loss or thinning concerns | May require gentler processing and specific product choices |

3. Tap **"Continue"**

### 3b. Photo Capture — Getting It Right

This is the most important step. The AI's accuracy depends entirely on photo quality.

**📷 The Perfect Hair Photo — Checklist:**

✅ **Natural daylight** — Position client near a window. No direct sunlight, but bright indirect light
✅ **6-8 inches away** — Close enough for detail, far enough for context
✅ **Clean, product-free hair** — Dry, no styling products (they alter appearance)
✅ **Even lighting across the head** — Avoid shadows on one side
✅ **Neutral background** — White or light gray wall behind the client

❌ **Avoid these common mistakes:**

- ❌ Harsh overhead salon lights (add false warmth/yellow cast)
- ❌ Flash-only photos (flatten the hair, distort color)
- ❌ Wet hair (water darkens the level by 1-2 levels — AI will read incorrectly)
- ❌ Product-heavy hair (oils, serums, shine sprays alter reflective properties)
- ❌ Dark backgrounds (confuse the AI's edge detection)
- ❌ Filters (any Instagram/Snapchat filter makes the photo useless)

**📸 Take 3 Photos:**

| Photo | What to Capture | How to Frame |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Top/Crown** | Overall natural level | Part hair down center, photograph from above at 45° angle |
| **Mid-lengths** | Tone and condition | Pull a section forward, photograph 6-8 inches away |
| **Ends** | Damage, buildup, fading | Spread ends flat, photograph from the side |

**💡 Pro Tips from Working Colorists:**

- "I take photos at the same window every time — consistency helps the AI learn my lighting conditions." — Sarah M., 12 years
- "If I'm unsure about a reading, I take a second photo from a slightly different angle. It helps confirm the level." — Marcus D., 8 years
- "For dark hair, I always get a mid-lengths shot with the hair spread flat — you can see the underlying pigment better that way." — Jen K., 15 years

### 3c. Select Desired Color

1. Choose your **color line** (e.g., Redken Shades EQ, Wella Koleston Perfect)
2. Browse or **search for a target shade** (e.g., "09V Platinum Ice")
3. The app shows the shade card with expected results

**Beta-supported lines:**
- Redken (Shades EQ, Color Gels Oils)
- Wella (Koleston Perfect, Color Touch)
- Schwarzkopf (IGORA Royal, IGORA Vibrance)
- Davines (A New Colour, View)
- L'Oréal Professionnel (Majirel, DIA Light)

### 3d. Review Your Formula

ColorGenius generates a personalized formulation card:

- **Primary shade** + ratio (e.g., 09V — 2 oz)
- **Secondary shade** + ratio (if mixing)
- **Developer** volume and amount
- **Processing time** with timer
- **Warnings** (e.g., "Pre-lightening recommended for levels 8+")
- **Confidence score** (how confident the AI is in this formula)

> **⚠️ Important:** You can adjust any component manually if your professional instinct disagrees — ColorGenius learns from your adjustments. Every override teaches the AI.

### 3e. Apply, Then Score

After your color service:

1. Return to the formulation
2. Tap **"Score Result"**
3. Take an **after photo** of the finished result (same lighting as the before photo)
4. ColorGenius compares before/after and scores:

| Category | What It Measures |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Color Accuracy** | How close to the target shade? (0-100%) |
| **Hair Condition** | Any visible damage, breakage, or compromise? |
| **Evenness** | Consistency from root to tip? |

5. Add your **personal notes** for next time

## Interpreting Results

### Understanding Your Formula Card

| Element | What It Means | When to Adjust |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Primary shade** | The dominant color tube | If you know the client's undertone pulls differently |
| **Secondary shade** | A modifier or tone adjuster | If you want warmer/cooler results than recommended |
| **Developer volume** | How much lift + deposit | For resistant hair, you may need to bump up; for fragile, go down |
| **Processing time** | How long to leave on | Adjust based on your experience with the client's hair type |
| **Confidence score** | AI's certainty (0-100%) | Scores below 70% mean the AI needs more data — trust your training |
| **Warnings** | Safety flags or special instructions | Never ignore warnings — they're based on the questionnaire |

### Confidence Scores Explained

| Score | Meaning | What to Do |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| **90-100%** | High confidence — strong data match | Proceed with confidence |
| **70-89%** | Good confidence — minor variables | Use your judgment on adjustments |
| **50-69%** | Moderate — unusual scenario or limited data | Treat as a starting point, rely on your training |
| **Below 50%** | Low confidence — edge case or insufficient data | Use as reference only, formulate manually |

### Result Scores — What They Mean

| Score | Interpretation |
|-------|---------------|
| **90-100% accuracy** | Target hit — formula worked as predicted |
| **75-89%** | Close result — minor adjustments for next time |
| **50-74%** | Noticeable gap — review the formulation notes and report back |
| **Below 50%** | Significant miss — report this to us immediately via the "Report Issue" button |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| **AI reads the level too dark** | Photo taken under warm/low light | Retake photo in natural daylight near a window |
| **AI reads the level too light** | Flash used, or hair was wet | Retake without flash, ensure hair is dry |
| **Wrong undertone detected** | Color-cast from background or lighting | Use a neutral background (white/gray wall) |
| **"Metallic dye detected" warning appears incorrectly** | Heavy product buildup resembling metallic sheen | Clarify the hair and retake; check client history |
| **No formulation generated** | Unsupported color line or incomplete questionnaire | Check that your color line is in beta; complete all questionnaire fields |
| **Confidence score is low** | Unusual combination of variables | This is normal for edge cases — use your expertise alongside the recommendation |
| **After-photo score seems wrong** | Different lighting than before photo | Always use the same lighting setup for before and after photos |
| **App is slow or unresponsive** | Internet connectivity | Check Wi-Fi; the app works best on stable connections |
| **Camera won't open** | Browser permissions | Allow camera access in your browser settings |
| **Formula looks wrong for the client** | Questionnaire may have missing info | Double-check all fields, especially treatment history and previous color |

**Still stuck?** Email beta@colorgenius.ai — we respond within 24 hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is ColorGenius a replacement for my color training?
**No.** ColorGenius augments your expertise. It handles the math and science — level systems, underlying pigments, developer chemistry — so you can focus on the art. Think of it as a calculator for colorists.

### 2. Which color lines are supported in beta?
Redken, Wella, Schwarzkopf, Davines, and L'Oréal Professionnel. We're adding more based on beta feedback. If your primary line isn't listed, tell us.

### 3. How accurate is the photo analysis?
**90%+ accuracy** on well-lit photos. Accuracy depends on lighting quality — natural daylight is best.

### 4. What happens if the formula is wrong?
Use the **"Report Issue"** button. Trust your training — override any component you disagree with. Every adjustment teaches the AI.

### 5. Is my client data private?
**Absolutely.** Client photos and questionnaires are encrypted and never shared. We use anonymized formulation data to improve the AI. Full privacy policy at colorgenius.ai/privacy.

### 6. Can I use ColorGenius for corrective color?
Yes, but with caveats. The questionnaire includes a "corrective color" option, and the AI accounts for previous services and buildup. Corrective work is the most complex scenario — use ColorGenius as a starting point.

### 7. What devices work?
Any modern browser — iPhone, iPad, Android, or desktop. Camera integration works on all devices. **Tablet recommended** for salon use.

### 8. How much does beta cost?
**Completely free.** Unlimited formulations, all color lines, full features. Beta testers get a **lifetime discount** on future paid plans.

### 9. What if my color line isn't supported?
Email beta@colorgenius.ai. We can typically add a new line within 2-4 weeks during beta.

### 10. How do I invite other stylists?
Have them email **beta@colorgenius.ai** with your name as a referral. We prioritize referred stylists for future cohorts.
