## 10. Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Characteristics
- **Confident** but not arrogant
- **Professional** but approachable
- **Technical** but explained simply
- **Excited** about results, not features

### Do's
- Use active voice
- Lead with benefits
- Be specific with numbers
- Address objections head-on
- Use "you" and "your"

### Don'ts
- Don't use jargon without explanation
- Don't make unsubstantiated claims
- Don't use passive voice
- Don't be overly formal
- Don't use exclamation points excessively

### Examples
| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|----------|--------|
| "Our AI uses neural networks" | "Our AI learns from 500K+ hair profiles to match your perfect color" |
| "Sign up for our service" | "Get your personalized color formula in 60 seconds" |
| "We offer color matching" | "Find your perfect color match — guaranteed" |
