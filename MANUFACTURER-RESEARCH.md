### Color Melt Technique
**Note:** Specific Color Melt formulas with shade numbers not found on official blog. Technique involves blending 3+ shades in seamless transition.

**General approach from Wella education:**
1. Section hair horizontally
2. Apply darkest shade at roots
3. Apply mid-tone to mid-lengths
4. Apply lightest tone to ends
5. Blend where colors meet
