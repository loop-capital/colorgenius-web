# ColorGenius Beta — Stylist Onboarding Guide

**Welcome to the future of hair color formulation.**

---

## What Is ColorGenius?

ColorGenius is an AI-powered hair color formulation tool that analyzes your client's hair through a photo, combines it with their treatment history, and delivers a precise formula — shades, developer, ratios, and processing time — in seconds. It's like having a senior colorist in your pocket, helping you nail color on the first try, every time.

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Access the App

ColorGenius runs in your browser — no app store download needed.

1. Open **app.colorgenius.ai** on your phone, tablet, or computer
2. Click **"Join Beta"**
3. Enter your invitation code (sent to your email)
4. Create your stylist profile:
   - Name
   - Salon name
   - Years of experience
   - Primary color line(s) you use
5. You're in! 🎉

**Recommended device:** Tablet (iPad or Android) for salon use — the camera integration works best on a device you can easily handhold near your station.

### Step 2: Set Up Your Profile

Before your first formulation, complete your preferences:

- **Primary color line:** Select the brand you use most (Redken, Wella, Schwarzkopf, etc.)
- **Default developer:** Set your preferred developer volumes
- **Units:** Choose oz or ml for measurements
- **Notification preferences:** Turn on/off result reminders

### Step 3: Your First Formulation

Here's a complete walkthrough for your first client:

#### 3a. Create a New Client

1. From the **Dashboard**, tap **"New Formulation"**
2. Fill in the **Client Questionnaire**:
   - **Treatment history:** Has the client had straightening, perm, or acid treatments? When?
   - **Metallic dye or henna:** Critical safety flag — be honest here
   - **Allergies:** Any known reactions to hair color products?
   - **Hair texture:** Fine, medium, or coarse
   - **Scalp condition:** Sensitive, normal, or other
   - **Previous color:** What brand? When? What service?
   - **Desired outcome:** Root touch-up, full color, highlights, corrective color?
   - **Hair loss concerns:** Any thinning to be aware of?
3. Tap **"Continue"**

#### 3b. Photo Capture

1. Position your client in **good, natural lighting** (near a window is ideal)
2. Hold the device **6-8 inches** from the hair
3. Capture **3 photos:**
   - **Top/crown** — shows overall level
   - **Mid-lengths** — shows tone and condition
   - **Ends** — shows damage or buildup
4. Alternatively, **upload existing photos** from your gallery
5. Tap **"Analyze"**

> **💡 Pro tip:** Avoid harsh overhead salon lights for photos — they distort warmth. Natural daylight gives the AI the most accurate read.

#### 3c. Select Desired Color

1. Choose your **color line** (e.g., Redken Shades EQ)
2. Browse or **search for a target shade** (e.g., "09V Platinum Ice")
3. The app will show the shade card with expected results

#### 3d. Review Your Formula

ColorGenius generates a personalized formulation card:

- **Primary shade** + ratio (e.g., 09V — 2 oz)
- **Secondary shade** + ratio (if mixing)
- **Developer** volume and amount
- **Processing time** with timer
- **Warnings** (e.g., "Pre-lightening recommended for levels 8+")
- **Confidence score** (how confident the AI is in this formula)

You can adjust any component manually if your professional instinct disagrees — ColorGenius learns from your adjustments.

#### 3e. Apply, Then Score

After your color service:

1. Return to the formulation
2. Tap **"Score Result"**
3. Take an **after photo** of the finished result
4. ColorGenius compares before/after and scores:
   - **Color accuracy:** How close to the target shade?
   - **Hair condition:** Any visible damage or compromise?
   - **Evenness:** Consistency from root to tip?
5. Add your **personal notes** for next time

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is ColorGenius a replacement for my color training?

**No.** ColorGenius is a tool that augments your expertise. It handles the math and science — level systems, underlying pigments, developer chemistry — so you can focus on the art. Think of it as a calculator for colorists: you still make the creative decisions.

### 2. Which color lines are supported in beta?

Beta supports **5 major lines:** Redken, Wella Koleston Perfect, Schwarzkopf IGORA, Davines, and L'Oréal Professionnel. We're adding more lines based on beta feedback. If your primary line isn't listed, tell us — that's exactly what we need to hear.

### 3. How accurate is the photo analysis?

Our AI analyzes hair level, tone, and condition from photos with **90%+ accuracy** on well-lit photos. Accuracy depends on lighting quality — natural daylight is best. Avoid heavy filters, flash-only shots, or photos taken under warm salon lighting (which adds false warmth readings).

### 4. What happens if the formula is wrong?

Two things: First, use the **"Report Issue"** button so we learn from it. Second, trust your training — ColorGenius recommends, but you're the professional. Override any component you disagree with. Every adjustment you make teaches the AI to improve for next time.

### 5. Is my client data private?

**Absolutely.** Client photos and questionnaires are encrypted and never shared. We use formulation data (anonymized) to improve the AI model, but no personally identifiable information leaves our servers. Full privacy policy at colorgenius.ai/privacy.

### 6. Can I use ColorGenius for corrective color?

**Yes, but with caveats.** The questionnaire includes a "corrective color" option, and the AI accounts for previous services and product buildup. However, corrective work is the most complex scenario — use ColorGenius as a starting point and apply your expertise from there.

### 7. What devices work with ColorGenius?

ColorGenius is a **web app** that works on any modern browser — iPhone, iPad, Android, or desktop. Camera integration works on all devices with a browser camera. For best experience, we recommend a **tablet** (iPad or Android) at your station.

### 8. How much does beta cost?

**Beta is completely free.** You get unlimited formulations, all supported color lines, and full feature access. After beta, there will be paid tiers — but beta testers get a **lifetime discount** as a thank-you for their early support.

### 9. What if my color line isn't supported?

Tell us immediately through the in-app feedback or email beta@colorgenius.ai. Our research team prioritizes new line integrations based on demand. We can typically add a new line within 2-4 weeks during beta.

### 10. How do I invite other stylists?

During beta, we're limiting access to 50 stylists to ensure quality. If you know someone who'd be perfect for the program, have them email **beta@colorgenius.ai** with your name as a referral. We'll prioritize referred stylists for future cohorts.
