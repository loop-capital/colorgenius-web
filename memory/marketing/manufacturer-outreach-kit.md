## Partnership Brief

### The Opportunity

Professional hair color is a **$2.4 billion** market in the US alone, yet stylists still rely on printed shade charts, memory, and trial-and-error to formulate color. The result? Inconsistent results, wasted product, and client dissatisfaction.

**ColorGenius is changing that.** Our AI-powered platform analyzes a client's hair through a photo, combines it with their treatment history and desired outcome, and delivers a precise formulation — in seconds.

### Why Partner with ColorGenius?

| For Manufacturers | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **Data at scale** | See which shades are actually used, how they're mixed, and what outcomes stylists achieve — aggregated, anonymized, and in real time |
| **Product development signals** | Identify formulation gaps, trending shades, and regional preferences before competitors |
| **Digital shelf presence** | Your color line featured in the formulation tool stylists reach for at every appointment |
| **Stylist loyalty** | Stylists using ColorGenius with your line develop deeper brand fluency and stick with your products |
| **Reduced education costs** | ColorGenius teaches correct usage of your products automatically — 24/7 AI coaching that reinforces your brand education |
| **First-mover advantage** | Early integration partners get preferred placement and co-development opportunities |

### What Integration Looks Like

**Level 1 — Data Partner (4-6 weeks)**
- ColorGenius ingests your shade catalog, mixing ratios, and technical data
- Your line appears in formulation recommendations
- You receive quarterly usage analytics reports

**Level 2 — Strategic Partner (8-12 weeks)**
- Everything in Level 1
- Co-branded marketing campaigns
- Early access to new ColorGenius features
- Joint educational content (formulation guides, technique videos)

**Level 3 — Preferred Partner (ongoing)**
- Everything in Level 2
- Featured placement in the app
- Co-developed features (e.g., brand-specific formulation modes)
- Revenue sharing on referrals
- White-label opportunities

### The Numbers

- **800,000** licensed cosmetologists in the US
- **480,000** active colorists (our serviceable market)
- **90%+** first-try formulation accuracy (our AI target)
- **50** beta stylists launching Aug 15, 2026 → **500** by end of year
- **3-5 color lines** supported at launch → scaling with partnerships

### What We Need From You

1. **Shade catalog data** — Complete shade charts, mixing ratios, developer compatibility
2. **Technical documentation** — Processing times, level systems, underlying pigment charts
3. **Brand guidelines** — Logo, colors, tone of voice for in-app representation
4. **Education resources** — Formulation guides, training materials (we digitize them into the AI)
5. **A point of contact** — Someone on your professional education or digital team who can answer technical questions quickly
