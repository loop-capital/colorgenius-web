## How It Works

### Step 1: Capture Your Client's Hair
**Photo → Analysis.** Take a photo of your client's hair (or upload one). Our AI reads the level, tone, and condition — accounting for previous treatments, texture, and any safety flags like metallic dye or henna.

### Step 2: Choose the Target Shade
**Select from your color line.** Pick the shade your client wants from your preferred brand's catalog. ColorGenius cross-references starting level, underlying pigment, and desired outcome to calculate the exact formulation.

### Step 3: Get Your Formula — and Score the Result
**Formulation → Application → Scoring.** Receive a complete formula card with mixing ratios, developer volume, and processing time. After the service, snap an after photo and our AI scores the result for accuracy, condition, and evenness — learning and improving with every use.

## Feature Highlights

### Feature 1: 🧠 AI Photo Analysis
**See what the eye sometimes misses.**
Our AI detects hair level, tone, porosity, and condition from a single photo. It flags safety concerns — metallic dye, henna, previous chemical treatments — before they become problems at the bowl.

### Feature 2: 🎯 Precision Formulation
**The right formula, every time.**
No more flipping through shade charts or relying on memory. ColorGenius calculates the exact shade mix, developer volume, and processing time based on 10+ variables — including your client's unique hair history and the specific color line you're using.

### Feature 3: 📊 Result Scoring & Learning
**Every formulation makes the next one better.**
After each service, our AI compares before and after photos, scoring color accuracy, hair condition, and application evenness. Over time, the engine learns from real-world results — your results — to deliver increasingly precise recommendations.

### Feature 4: 🔒 Client History & Safety
**Never start from scratch.**
Every client's questionnaire, photo, formulation, and result is saved to their profile. Returning clients? Their complete color history is one tap away — including safety flags, previous services, and what worked (or didn't).

## FAQ Section (Landing Page — Abbreviated)

**Is ColorGenius only for new stylists?**
No. ColorGenius helps stylists at every level — from recent graduates building confidence to veterans handling complex corrective color. The AI adapts to your experience.

**Which color lines do you support?**
Redken, Wella, Schwarzkopf, Davines, and L'Oréal Professionnel at launch, with more lines added based on demand.

**Does it replace my color training?**
ColorGenius is a tool, not a teacher. It handles the calculation so you can focus on the art. Think of it as a calculator for colorists.

**Is client data secure?**
Yes. All photos and client data are encrypted and never shared. We use anonymized formulation data to improve the AI — no personal information.

**What does "AI-powered" actually mean?**
Our AI analyzes hair photos to detect level and tone, combines that with your client's treatment history, and calculates a precise formula using your preferred color line's technical data. It also learns from results over time.
