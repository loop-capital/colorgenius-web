# Color Genius - Go-to-Market Strategy

## Executive Summary

Color Genius enters a $2.4B addressable market with a differentiated AI-powered solution. This document outlines our sales strategy, partnership approach, launch plan, and revenue projections to achieve market leadership in professional hair color technology.

---

## Market Analysis

### Target Market

**Primary Market:** Licensed Professional Stylists
- **US Market Size:** 800,000 licensed cosmetologists
- **Serviceable Addressable:** 480,000 (active colorists)
- **Target Penetration:** 1-5% (Year 1-3)

**Secondary Market:** Salons & Schools
- ~85,000 salon establishments in US
- 1,500+ cosmetology schools

**Tertiary Market:** Color Brands & Distributors
- 10+ major professional color lines
- Distributors serving 50,000+ salons

### Market Segmentation

```
Stylist Segmentation:
├── New Stylists (0-2 years)
│   ├── 160,000 stylists (20%)
│   ├── Pain: Learning formulation
│   ├── Value: Education, confidence
│   └── Price Sensitivity: High
│
├── Growing Stylists (2-5 years)
│   ├── 240,000 stylists (30%)
│   ├── Pain: Efficiency, building clientele
│   ├── Value: Speed, consistency
│   └── Price Sensitivity: Medium
│
├── Established Stylists (5-15 years)
│   ├── 280,000 stylists (35%)
│   ├── Pain: Keeping up with trends
│   ├── Value: Advanced techniques, client mgmt
│   └── Price Sensitivity: Low
│
└── Master Stylists (15+ years)
    ├── 120,000 stylists (15%)
    ├── Pain: Mentoring, scaling knowledge
       ├── Value: Teaching tool, salon growth
    └── Price Sensitivity: Very Low
```

### Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Type | Strengths | Weaknesses | Our Advantage |
|------------|------|-----------|------------|---------------|
| **Redken Shade Charts** | Traditional | Brand trust | Static, no learning | AI-powered, adaptive |
| **Wella Color Touch** | Traditional | Accuracy | Manual lookup | Instant, photo-based |
| **Hair Color Converter Apps** | Basic apps | Free | Limited features | Comprehensive platform |
| **SalonScale** | Business tool | Cost tracking | No formulation | End-to-end color science |
| **Vish** | Color management | Inventory mgmt | Expensive, complex | Simpler, AI-first |

**Key Differentiators:**
1. AI photo analysis (no manual color matching)
2. 10+ variable formulation (vs. 3-4 in competitors)
3. Learning system that improves with use
4. Direct brand integration
5. Affordable pricing for individual stylists

## Sales Strategy

### Direct-to-Stylist (B2C)

**Channels:**
1. **Instagram/TikTok** - Primary discovery
2. **YouTube** - Tutorials, testimonials
3. **Cosmetology schools** - Early career adoption
4. **Industry publications** - Credibility
5. **Referral program** - Word of mouth

**Tactics:**
- **Influencer partnerships:** Partner with colorist influencers
- **Before/after content:** Showcase real transformations
- **Educational content:** Color theory, technique tips
- **User-generated content:** Stylists share their Color Genius formulas
- **Free trial:** 14-day full access

### Salon Sales (B2B Small)

**Target:** Independent salons, 3-20 stylists

**Approach:**
1. **Land:** Get 1-2 stylists using (individual accounts)
2. **Expand:** Offer salon upgrade
3. **Multi-location:** Support chains

**Sales Motion:**
- Self-serve: Online signup
- Inside sales: For 10+ seats
- Live demos: Weekly webinars

### Enterprise Sales (B2B Large)

**Target:**
- Salon chains (50+ locations)
- Color brand distributors
- Cosmetology schools
- Franchise systems

**Sales Process:**
```
Week 1-2: Discovery & qualification
Week 3-4: Pilot setup (single location)
Week 5-8: Pilot execution
Week 9-10: Evaluation & expansion planning
Week 11-12: Contract negotiation
Week 13+: Rollout
```

**Average Deal Size:**
- Small chain (10-50 locations): $15K-50K/year
- Medium chain (50-200): $50K-200K/year
- Large chain (200+): $200K-500K+/year
- Brand partnerships: $100K-1M+/year

### LTV Optimization

**LTV Formula:**
```
LTV = ARPU × Gross Margin × Customer Lifespan

Professional Tier:
- ARPU: $35/month (blended)
- Gross Margin: 75%
- Lifespan: 18 months
- LTV: $35 × 0.75 × 18 = $472

Salon Tier:
- ARPU: $200/month
- Gross Margin: 80%
- Lifespan: 24 months
- LTV: $200 × 0.80 × 24 = $3,840
```

**LTV:CAC Ratios:**
- Individual: 11.8:1 (target > 3:1)
- Salon: 38.4:1 (target > 5:1)
